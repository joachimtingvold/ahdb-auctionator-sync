# Changelog

## v0.3.0-beta — 2026-11-15

Initial public version for WoW: Forever (Interface 16001).

- Synchronize completed AHDB faction full scans into Auctionator's persistent price database.
- Preserve Auctionator daily history and update current minimums where supported.
- Keep neutral AH scans separate from faction markets.
- Add manual import of stored AHDB history with a configurable age limit.
- Provide `/ahsync` status, enable/disable and import-age commands.
- Verified in-game: `/ahsync import` and the automatic trigger after a completed AHDB scan.
